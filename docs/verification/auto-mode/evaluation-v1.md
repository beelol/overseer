## Routes and reference behavior

The fixture provider exposes `general-A` at medium effort, `general-B` at medium effort, and `frontier-C` at high effort. They use independent fixture accounts with 100 draw units each and known active windows. `general-A` and `frontier-C` have the browser tool; `general-B` does not. All three support workspace-write approval policy, but only `frontier-C` is verified for the diagnosis's frontier requirement. A fourth `local-D` route has no demonstrated browser or diagnosis capability; zero subscription draw cannot make it eligible for those tasks. The selector must receive these capabilities and meter readings through the same normalized route and observation interfaces used by normal Auto decisions, never through a test-only provider ranking file.

The reference baseline always chooses the most expensive *eligible* route, `frontier-C`, for each unit. It completes all three. The candidate Auto run starts from an empty learning history and may use only prior ordinary fixture work for any later adaptation. The held-out outcomes and charges are revealed to the evaluator after each selected unit settles, not to the selector beforehand.

Each coordinator assessment costs 1 synthetic draw unit, even if it produces no child. A cross-harness handoff after partial execution costs 1 additional unit for context transfer and validation; a same-harness continuation after partial execution costs 0.5. Ordinary initial managed-child workspaces are included in the route charges above. A replayed or interrupted attempt still counts its incurred charge. If the selected route fails and a retry, verification run, escalation, or handoff is needed, include *all* of those charges in the selected total. The baseline total is 31 units: 9 + 8 + 11 for the three units and 3 for their assessments; it has no handoff. Baseline quality is 3/3 completed units.

## Frozen pass threshold

All three mandatory completion checks must pass. A rubric score gives each unit 1 only when its entire check passes, otherwise 0; Auto must score 3/3, so a cheap but inadequate diagnosis cannot hide behind cheaper browser work. Total selected draw, including assessments, failed attempts, corrections, verification, and handoffs, must be at most **24.8 units** (80% of the 31-unit reference baseline). Each selected route must be eligible from evidence available *before* launch, and the evaluator must verify no duplicate work-unit execution, permission bypass, fabricated allowance, or unrecorded draw. If a test case cannot be exercised with the actual daemon/adapters, report it as missing coverage rather than a pass.
