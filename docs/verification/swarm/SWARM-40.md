# SWARM-40 — the run's allocation from a percentage allowance

Status: partial (2026-09-28, `claude/auto-swarm`). Everything in the clause is covered at fixture scope, including a calibrated director's booking drawing on the run's allocation. The gap is an uncalibrated director, or one whose draw exceeds the allocation: it still runs unbooked, so its turns are not metered.

Criterion: with 60 percentage points of fresh unreserved/unprotected remaining weekly allowance and no override, derive at most 6 points for the run and a minimum 1.2-point finishing reserve. Apply a tighter short-window limit independently. Missing compatible worker estimates prevents default fan-out; no token conversion is fabricated. Planning, routing inference, messaging, reproduction and replacements share the original allocation.

What is left (2026-09-28, `claude/auto-swarm`): a director whose `swarm/director` draw is unknown (an uncalibrated account) or larger than the run's remaining allocation still launches unbooked, as an ordinary start does, so its turns are not metered to the allocation. Closing it needs a choice the owner has not made: either refuse worker fan-out while the run's director is unmetered (every real account starts uncalibrated, so this would hold fan-out until calibration), or refuse the director's launch when its known draw exceeds the allocation. The rest of the clause is covered at fixture scope, and live calibration on the owner's personal Claude account is still owed.
