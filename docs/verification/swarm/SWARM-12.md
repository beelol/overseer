# SWARM-12 — unknown and estimated quota

Status: verified for deterministic admission and status behavior with injected quota observations. Support level: fixture snapshots and the existing Swarm sidebar; live provider collection and native usage settlement remain SWARM-13/24 gaps.

Input: a two-job category receives an unknown balance, an expired snapshot, then a fresh estimated 100,000 milli-point balance. A default run has no estimate permission. A second run explicitly saves `allow_estimated_quota=true`, observes the same estimated window and reserves 100 milli-points for its first job. The owner revokes that active run's estimate permission, restarts the daemon, and requests its second job with the old estimate. The fixture then supplies a new estimated observation and, separately, fresh exact quota.

Expected: unknown, stale and unpermitted estimated quota cannot admit workers or appear as zero/unlimited. An expressly permitted estimate may admit within the frozen 10% allocation, with its confidence shown in status. Revocation is durable and blocks further estimated admission, while fresh exact capacity can be reassessed and used without restoring estimate permission. The earlier reservation remains recorded.

Observed: admission returns `unknown_quota`, `stale_snapshot` and `estimated_quota_requires_permission` in the default run. Its capacity reports provider usage `unknown` and no invented allocation. The permitted run's availability window reports `confidence=estimated`, its first 100 milli-point reservation is admitted under a 10,000 milli-point frozen allocation, and a recorded revocation survives restart. The next admission is held. Reobserving estimated quota reports `estimated_quota_requires_permission`; reobserving exact quota permits a second job without reopening the estimate grant. The sidebar displays `estimated` next to a numeric allowance without requiring a hover; unknown allowance is labelled unknown and expired observations are marked expired. The focused test was red before the revocation method existed and green after implementation.

Evidence: `daemon/tests/swarm_policy.rs` (`unknown_stale_or_incompatible_units_do_not_enable_fanout`, `affected_targets_fail_independently_and_estimated_permission_is_explicit`), `daemon/tests/swarm_admission.rs` (`estimated_quota_requires_run_permission_and_revocation_blocks_future_jobs`), `test/unit/swarm-view.js`, `daemon/src/swarm/{settings,availability,admission,policy,schema}.rs`, and `extension/src/views.js`.

Verification: `cargo test -p overseerd --offline --test swarm_admission -q` (39 passed), `cargo test -p overseerd --offline --test swarm_policy -q` (11 passed), `cargo test -p overseerd --offline --test swarm_settings -q` (6 passed), `npm run check` and `npm test` in `extension/` (5 of 5 unit files passed), and `git diff --check`.

Boundary: these are local, unpaid observations supplied by a fixture. The live Auto Mode feed, shared ordinary/Auto/Swarm admission authority and measured settlement are still unverified under SWARM-13/24 and CONTRACT-01. The revocation control is protective; a changed saved application or category preset alone does not retroactively change an active run's permission.
